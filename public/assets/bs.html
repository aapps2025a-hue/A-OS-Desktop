Welcome to A-OS BS

A classic desktop for your A-OS Clowd backups.

Desktop and windows

Double-click desktop icons, or click shelf icons. Drag title bars to move windows. Use the bottom-right corner to resize. − minimises, □ maximises, and × closes. Click a shelf icon to restore a window.

Files and Clowd

Upload selected files or a folder, create text/JSON/CSV/SQL files, and download backups. Every save creates a separate copy. Files appear after you log in with the same A-Apps account. Firebase Storage must be configured using the instructions at the top of this HTML source.

Camera and ScreenSnap

Start the camera or choose a screen, window or tab to capture. Your browser will ask for permission. Capture previews remain on this device until you choose Save to Clowd. HTTPS or localhost is required; screen capture support varies by browser.

Media Files

Open local photos, audio and video, or choose a media backup in Files. Playback depends on formats supported by your browser.
